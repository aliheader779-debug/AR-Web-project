What Is Udemy?

Udemy is an online course marketplace founded in 2010. Unlike university platforms, anyone with real expertise can publish a course — so the instructors are usually working developers, designers, marketers, and freelancers teaching the exact skills they use daily. The library holds more than 200,000 video courses across programming, design, business, marketing, IT, personal development, and dozens of other topics, taught by tens of thousands of instructors and taken by millions of students worldwide. Courses are typically bought once and kept with lifetime access, which means you can start, pause, and return to a course whenever you like — there is no semester and no expiry pressure.

Who Should Use Udemy?

  • Self-learners — pick any skill and start today, no admission needed
  • Freelancers — add billable skills like web development, UI design, or video editing
  • Career switchers — move into tech or marketing with affordable, practical courses
  • Students — learn tools your degree does not cover, like Excel automation or Python
  • Hobby learners — photography, music, cooking, languages — Udemy covers it all
💡 Tip: Before starting any course, check its rating and read a few recent reviews. A course with 4.6+ stars and thousands of students is usually a safe pick — and watch the free preview lessons first.
