What Do You Get with Framer?

Framer combines design freedom with real publishing — what you draw is what goes live:

  • Visual website builder — design pages on a canvas with full control over layout, typography and spacing, the way a designer thinks, not the way a template forces.
  • CMS for site content — manage blog posts, portfolios, listings or menus as structured content, so updating the site does not mean redesigning it.
  • Responsive breakpoints — tune how the layout adapts across desktop, tablet and phone from the same design file.
  • Built-in animations and effects — scroll effects, page transitions and micro-interactions are part of the builder, not plugins you must install and maintain.
  • Custom domains — connect your own domain so the site lives at your address, not a builder subdomain.
  • SEO settings — control page titles, descriptions and indexing basics so the site can be found on Google.
  • Starter templates — begin from professionally designed templates and customise them, instead of starting from a blank page.
  • Publishing and hosting — sites are published and hosted from the same platform, with secure HTTPS included.

How a Framer Project Works, Step by Step

A typical small business website in Framer moves like this:

  1. Pick a starting point. Choose a template close to what you need — a portfolio, a business site, a landing page — or start blank.
  2. Design the pages. Edit text, images, colours and sections visually. What you see on the canvas is the actual website.
  3. Add content with the CMS. Blog posts or portfolio items are entered once as content entries and appear automatically in the right layouts.
  4. Check every screen size. Switch between breakpoints and adjust the mobile layout until it looks right on a phone.
  5. Connect the domain and publish. Point your domain, adjust the SEO titles, and publish — the site is live on secure hosting.

There is no separate developer step: the person who designs the site is the person who publishes it.
