Canva is the design tool behind almost every Pakistani business post, thumbnail and resume you see online.   • Premium templates for posts, reels, presentations and resumes

  • Brand kit — save your logos, colors and fonts
  • Premium stock photos, videos and elements unlocked

  • Designs stay in your own Canva account — invite sirf premium kholta hai

Kis ke liye hai ye? Students jo presentations aur assignments ko professional banana chahte hain; freelancers jo clients ke liye posts aur logos banate hain; chhote business jo apni dukaan ya page ki rozana promotion khud design karte hain; aur content creators jin ke thumbnails hi un ki pehchan hain. Agar aap in mein se kisi ek bhi hain, to free version ki rukawatain aap roz mehsoos kar rahay honge — Edu un sab ka hal hai. Aur agar aap ka kaam video ki taraf ziada hai, to CapCut Pro ke saath ye jodi (design + video) aksar creators ki pehli pasand hoti hai.
