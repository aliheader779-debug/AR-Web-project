How to Get Gemini Pro Free in Pakistan (Students)

Pakistani students ke liye 2026 ki sab se achi khabar: Google ne Pakistan mein students ko Google AI Plus (Gemini Pro) 12 months ke liye bilkul FREE dena shuru kar diya hai. Ye offer 19 August 2026 ko Pakistan–Google MoU ke baad live hui — lekin is ki last date 31 December 2026 hai. Is ke baad ye offer khatam ho jayegi. Is guide mein poora tareeqa step by step hai: kaun eligible hai, claim kaise karna hai, aur woh ek zaroori baat jo aksar koi nahi batata — 12 months ke baad kya hota hai.

⏰ Deadline yaad rakhein: Offer sirf 31 December 2026 tak claim ki ja sakti hai. Jitni jaldi claim karein ge, utne ziada mahine free milen ge — 12 months activation ki date se shuru hote hain.

Free Offer Mein Kya Kya Milta Hai?

Google is offer ko "Google AI Plus student offer" kehta hai — is mein Gemini Pro ki full access shamil hai. Free 12 months mein ye sab milta hai:

  • Gemini Pro ki expanded access — Google ke sab se capable AI models, free version se kahin ziada usage limits ke saath.
  • 400 GB cloud storage — Google Drive, Gmail aur Photos ke liye — students ke liye ye akela faida bhi bohat bara hai.
  • Study tools — study notebooks, flashcards aur practice quizzes: apne lecture notes aur slides upload karein aur un se tayyar shuda study material banwayein.
  • Gemini Live — bol kar sawal poochein aur jawab sunein, jaise kisi teacher se baat ho rahi ho.
  • AI image aur video features — presentations aur projects ke liye visuals banane mein madad.

Matlab ye sirf ek chatbot nahi — poora study package hai. Assignment, research, exam revision aur presentation — har kaam mein kaam aata hai.

Kaun Eligible Hai? — Checklist

Claim karne se pehle ye 5 cheezein check kar lein — agar ye sab hain to aap eligible hain:

  • ✔ Higher-education student hon — kisi recognized university ya college mein enrolled hon (matric/inter ke students is offer mein nahi aate).
  • ✔ Umar 18 saal ya us se ziada ho.
  • ✔ Pakistan mein hon — ye offer Pakistan ke liye khaas tor par live hai.
  • ✔ Personal Google account ho — university ka email account NAHI chalega, apna personal Gmail chahiye.
  • ✔ Valid payment method ho — signup par card add karna zaroori hai (neeche parhein, charge nahi hota).

Claim Karne Ke Steps — Step by Step

Poora process taqreeban 10–15 minute ka hai:

  1. Offer page kholein: one.google.com/ai-student par jayein.
  2. Personal Gmail se sign in karein: Apne personal Google account se login karein — university wala account istemal na karein.
  3. Student offer select karein: Page par "Claim a student plan" ya student offer wala option dikhega — use select karein.
  4. Student status verify karwayein: Google aap ko SheerID verification par bhejega. Wahan apni university ka naam likhein aur enrollment certificate ya student ID upload karein. Verification aam tor par kuch hi minute mein ho jati hai — kabhi kabhi mazeed maloomat bhi mangi ja sakti hai, is ka matlab reject hona nahi hota.
  5. Payment method add karein: Ek valid card add karna zaroori hai — free period mein is se koi charge NAHI hota, ye sirf is liye manga jata hai taake 12 months ke baad subscription chal sake (agar aap cancel na karein).
  6. Activate karein: Confirm karte hi subscription foran active ho jati hai — Gemini app ya website par Pro features dikhne lagen ge.
💡 Tip: Verification se pehle apna student ID ya enrollment certificate tayyar rakhein — aur wohi naam aur email likhein jo aap ke university record mein hai. Naam mein farq verification atakne ki sab se aam wajah hai.

⚠️ Sab Se Zaroori Baat — 12 Months Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai?

Ye woh hissa hai jo aksar koi nahi batata: 12 free months khatam hote hi subscription khud ba khud paid plan mein badal jati hai — Pakistan mein is waqt Google AI Plus ki qeemat Rs. 1,400/month hai, aur ye aap ke card se charge hone lagti hai. Is se bachne ke liye:

  • Activation ki date note karein — screenshot le lein ya calendar mein likh lein.
  • 11 months baad reminder lagayein — phone par alarm ya Google Calendar reminder set karein.
  • Cancel karna asaan hai: myaccount.google.com/subscriptions par jayein, plan select karein aur cancel kar dein — 2 minute ka kaam hai.

Agar aap ko Pro pasand aye aur continue karna ho to kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi — subscription khud chalti rahegi. Lekin agar free period ke baad nahi chahiye to reminder lagana mat bhooliye ga.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gemini Pro really free for students in Pakistan?
Yes — eligible higher-education students can claim Google AI Plus (which includes Gemini Pro access) free for 12 months through Google's official student offer at one.google.com/ai-student. You must redeem it by 31 December 2026, verify your student status through SheerID, and add a valid payment method at signup.

Do I need a payment method if the offer is free?
Yes. Google asks for a valid payment method at signup even though the 12 months are free. Nothing is charged during the free period — but after 12 months the subscription auto-renews at the standard price (currently PKR 1,400/month in Pakistan) unless you cancel first.

Can I use my university email to claim the offer?
No — Google requires a personal Google account for the student offer, not a school-issued account. Your student status is verified separately through SheerID using your enrollment certificate or student ID.

What happens after the free 12 months end?
The subscription automatically converts to a paid Google One plan at the standard price (currently PKR 1,400/month in Pakistan) unless you cancel. Set a reminder for 11 months after activation and cancel at myaccount.google.com/subscriptions if you do not want to continue.

What if I am not a student — can I still get Gemini Pro cheap?
Yes. At AR SERVICES, Gemini AI Pro is available for Rs. 250 for 1 month or Rs. 400 for 2 months, activated on your own Google account — no student verification and no payment card needed.
