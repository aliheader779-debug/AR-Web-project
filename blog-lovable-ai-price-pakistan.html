What Is Lovable?

Lovable (lovable.dev) is an AI app builder that went from launch to breakout success in months. The idea is simple: instead of writing code, you write a description — "build me a restaurant website with online table booking and a menu page" — and Lovable generates a complete working web application: the pages, the design, the database, and the login system. Under the hood, it produces React and Tailwind CSS frontends and connects to Supabase for the backend (database, authentication, storage). That means what you get is not a mockup or a template — it is a real application you can publish, share with customers, and keep improving by chatting with the AI: "make the header darker," "add a contact form," "add a payments page." Changes apply live and you can publish updates with one click. Lovable also syncs with GitHub, so a developer can take over the code later if your project grows — you are never locked into a dead end.

Tips for Your First Lovable Project

Start small and specific. A prompt like "build an app" is too vague — the AI guesses, and you get something generic. A prompt like "build a one-page website for my bakery in Mirpur with a menu section, a WhatsApp order button, and a photo gallery" gives the builder a clear target. Describe the pages you want, the colours you like, and what each button should do. After the first version appears, improve it in rounds: fix the layout first, then add features, then polish the wording. One change at a time keeps the AI focused and saves your credits. And before you publish anything customer-facing, click through every page on your phone — most of your visitors will open it on mobile, not a laptop.
