What Is LinkedIn?

LinkedIn is the world's largest professional network, launched in 2003 and now used by more than a billion members across 200+ countries. It is where professionals build a public CV, connect with colleagues, follow companies, and — most importantly — where recruiters actively search for candidates. In Pakistan, LinkedIn has become the standard channel for white-collar hiring: HR teams in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad, plus remote employers and Gulf recruiters, all search LinkedIn before they post a job ad anywhere else. A complete, active profile is no longer optional for serious job seekers — it is the first impression.

What Is LinkedIn Premium Career?

LinkedIn Premium Career is LinkedIn's job-seeker subscription: a toolkit that sits on top of the free professional network and gives you advantages at every step of the job hunt. It is built for people who are actively applying — fresh graduates, job switchers, and anyone who wants recruiters to notice them first.

What Premium Career Adds

  • Profile view insights — see the full list of who viewed your profile, so you know which recruiters and companies are checking you out
  • Applicant insights — compare yourself with other applicants on skills, experience, and seniority before you apply
  • InMail messages — message recruiters and hiring managers directly, even if you are not connected
  • AI-assisted applications — AI help with your profile and applications so you present yourself at your best
  • LinkedIn Learning — full access to thousands of professional courses in business, technology, and creative skills
  • Featured applicant status — stand out when you apply to jobs recruiters are actively watching
