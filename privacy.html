Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information AR SERVICES collects when you order, and how it is used. AR SERVICES is operated by Ali Haider.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address, which you enter in the order form. The form opens WhatsApp with your order message, so this information reaches us through WhatsApp.
  • Order details (product, plan) and the payment confirmation you send us on WhatsApp, such as a payment screenshot.
  • Any account detail a product needs for activation, for example the email address an invitation or activation must be sent to.

How we use it

  • Only to process and deliver your order, provide support and warranty/replacement help, and send renewal reminders for your own subscriptions.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except where delivery of your order requires it (for example sending a team invitation to your email).

Your choices

You can ask us on WhatsApp (+92 317 9172511) at any time to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and to stop renewal reminders. This website loads fonts and libraries from public content networks (such as Google Fonts and jsDelivr); those services may receive standard technical request data such as your IP address.
