Canva Edu vs Canva Pro — Difference & Which One Should You Buy?

Team AR SERVICES23 October 20266 min readLast updated: 23 October 2026
Canva

"Canva Edu loon ya Canva Pro?" — ye woh sawal hai jo har doosra customer hum se poochta hai. Dono ke naam sun kar lagta hai ke Pro "asal" cheez hogi aur Edu us ka halka version — lekin asal haqiqat is se mukhtalif hai. Is guide mein hum dono ka seedha muqabla kar rahay hain: features, istemal ka tareeqa, aur qeemat — taake aap apne kaam ke hisaab se sahi faisla kar saken. Sirf qeemat dekhni hai to hamari Canva price guide maujood hai; yahan faisla features par hoga.

Dono Hain Kya? — Buniyadi Farq

Canva Pro Canva ka personal premium subscription hai — aap seedha Canva se apne account ke liye kharidte hain, aur har mahina ya saal ki qeemat ada karte hain. Canva Edu ek education team workspace ke zariye milta hai: aap ko ek team ka invite ata hai, aap apni email se use join karte hain, aur premium features aap ke apne account mein khul jate hain. Designs, files aur uploads aap ke apne account mein hi bante aur rehte hain — team sirf premium access ka darwaza hai. AR SERVICES par Canva isi Edu tareeqay se milta hai, kyun ke Pakistan mein ziada tar users ke liye yehi qeemat ke hisaab se sahi model hai.

To Phir Konsa Lena Chahiye?

Canva Edu lein agar: aap student, freelancer, chhota business ya page admin hain; aap ko premium templates, background remover aur AI tools chahiye; aur aap har mahina bari qeemat nahi dena chahte. Ye Pakistan ke 90% users ke liye sahi jawab hai — isi liye AR SERVICES ke Canva plans hamare sab se ziada बिकne walay plans mein hain.

Canva Pro tab sochein agar: aap ko apna bilkul personal, apne naam ka subscription chahiye jis ka pura control sirf aap ke paas ho, aur aap us ki kai guna ziada qeemat dene ke liye taiyar hain. Bari agencies aur woh professionals jin ke clients ko personal workspace darkar ho, un ke liye ye samajh ata hai — baaqi sab ke liye Edu ka premium tajurba usi kaam ko bohat kam qeemat mein de deta hai.

Ek aur seedhi baat: kuch log samajhte hain ke Edu mein designs mehfooz nahi rehte. Aisa nahi hai — aap jo kuch banate hain woh aap ke apne Canva account mein hota hai; invite sirf premium features kholta hai. Isi liye hum hamesha kehte hain: invite usi email par lein jo aap rozana istemal karte hain.

💡 Tip: Agar aap designs ka kaam lagataar karte hain — page, clients ya apna business — to 3 Months wala plan lein. Rs. 250 mein teen mahine ka sukoon, aur mahina khatam honay ki tension khatam.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Canva Edu the same as Canva Pro?
For everyday design work, the experience is almost the same: premium templates, background remover, brand kit and AI tools are included in both. The difference is the model — Edu comes through an education team workspace, Pro is a personal subscription that costs many times more.

Do my designs stay in my own account with Canva Edu?
Yes. The team invite only unlocks premium features — everything you create stays in your own Canva account under your own email.

Which one should a freelancer or student buy?
For students and freelancers, Canva Edu is the smart choice — the same premium design tools at a small fraction of the personal Pro price.
